# DaryeelPass Hargeisa Directory and Booking Request Demo

**Status:** Local/portfolio demo, October 2026

## Product summary

DaryeelPass helps people in Hargeisa discover clinic contact information and submit booking requests inside the app. The first version uses email/password accounts, suggested times generated by DaryeelPass, and times entered by invited clinic staff. Requests remain in DaryeelPass and are not sent to clinic systems.

## User roles

- **Patient:** register with email and password, browse clinics, request a time with a contact phone number, and track or cancel pending requests.
- **Staff:** accept a one-time clinic-admin invite for one clinic, enter availability, and help process requests.
- **Clinic admin:** manage availability and booking requests for one assigned clinic, and invite staff for that clinic.
- **System admin:** created through a setup-key protected first-run flow with a persistent completion marker; oversee all clinics, requests, and account counts; invite, disable, and re-enable clinic admins.

Patient signup creates patient accounts only. Roles and clinic assignments cannot be selected during signup. The `SYSTEM_SETUP_KEY` must be a unique secret value; after initial setup it can be removed from the server environment. Signup does not verify email. Email delivery, verification, password recovery, clinic system integration, and production identity are out of scope.

## Availability and request semantics

- Generated time suggestions are explicitly marked as unconfirmed and do not represent clinic schedules.
- Clinic staff can enter schedule times. A request against a staff-entered time still does not reserve the clinic's real-world schedule.
- Requests have `PENDING`, `CONFIRMED`, `DECLINED`, and `CANCELLED` states. `CONFIRMED` means staff marked it confirmed inside DaryeelPass only; it is not proof of confirmation in a clinic's system.
- Requests store account, clinic, service, requested time, and contact phone. No medical reason, diagnosis, national identifier, or health history is collected.

## Data and security design

- PostgreSQL stores user accounts, scrypt password hashes, hashed revocable session tokens, one-time clinic-scoped staff invites, availability, and booking requests.
- Patient and staff API queries apply route authorization and transaction-local PostgreSQL context for row-level security policies.
- The API uses a restricted, non-owner, `NOBYPASSRLS` runtime database role; schema migrations use a separate owner URL.
- Account signup assigns only the patient role. Staff membership is assigned by admin invite; public self-selection of role or clinic is not allowed.
- Public clinic listing fields remain readable without an account.

## Not included

- Clinic partnerships, live schedules, actual reservations, clinic notifications, or real booking confirmations.
- Email verification, password reset delivery, production availability, or use with real patient or identity data.
- Medical advice, recommendations, records, or emergency-care workflows.

## Next phase before public or clinical use

Obtain clinic authorization and integration agreements; implement real schedule ownership and booking confirmation; add verified email and account recovery; review privacy, security, data retention, support, and legal obligations; and verify deployment roles, secrets, HTTPS, backups, and operational monitoring. Continue using synthetic data until then.
